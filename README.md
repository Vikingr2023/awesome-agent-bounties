### 任务描述

## Objective

Collect, preserve, and compare AGI architecture proposals generated by multiple AI systems. The goal is not to paste generic AI answers, but to produce an auditable research packet that makes different architecture ideas comparable and useful for Cognitive-OS planning.

## Reward

$3,000 USD for an accepted submission.

Payment details will be handled privately after acceptance. The maintainer may accept one winning submission, request revisions before acceptance, or reject submissions that do not meet the criteria below.

## Required Scope

Use at least 8 distinct AI systems or model families where possible. Examples may include ChatGPT/OpenAI models, Claude, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, Qwen, Llama-based systems, Mistral-based systems, Perplexity, or other publicly accessible AI tools.

For each system, collect an AGI architecture proposal using a comparable prompt. The prompt may be adapted per model only when necessary, but the changes must be documented.

## Deliverables

Submit a pull request that adds a research packet under:

`research/ai_generated_agi_architectures/`

Required files:

- `README.md`: overview of the packet, collection method, and headline findings.
- `prompts.md`: the exact prompts or prompt summaries used for each model.
- `raw_outputs/`: one file per AI system containing the raw or minimally cleaned architecture proposal.
- `comparison.csv`: a structured comparison across architecture dimensions.
- `summary.md`: synthesis of common patterns
